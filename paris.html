ABOUT PARIS

A City of Art, Romance & Timeless Elegance

Located in northern France, Paris is celebrated for its beautiful architecture, historic monuments, artistic heritage and charming neighbourhoods. From riverside walks along the Seine to cosy cafes and world-renowned museums, Paris offers an unforgettable European experience.

Paris at a Glance

A few things to know before you explore.

Best Time to Visit

April – June and September – October for pleasant weather and sightseeing.

Currency

Euro (€).

Language

French. English is spoken in many tourist areas.

Famous Experiences

Art museums, iconic landmarks, river cruises, shopping and French cuisine.

TRAVEL SMART

Tips for Your Paris Adventure

A little planning goes a long way.

Getting Around

Use the Metro, buses and regional trains to explore Paris conveniently.

Accommodation

Choose from boutique hotels, charming apartments and centrally located stays.

Budget Planning

Plan ahead for museum tickets, transport, meals and popular attractions.

What to Pack

Carry comfortable walking shoes, layered clothing and an umbrella for unexpected rain.

Winter
