ABOUT SWITZERLAND

A Land of Mountains, Lakes & Timeless Beauty

Nestled in the heart of Europe, Switzerland is known for its breathtaking alpine landscapes, crystal-clear lakes, charming villages and unforgettable experiences.

PLAN YOUR JOURNEY

Switzerland at a Glance

A few things to know before you explore.

Best Time to Visit

May – September for hiking and sightseeing. December – March for snow activities.

Currency

Swiss Franc (CHF)

Languages

German, French, Italian and Romansh.

Famous Experiences

Scenic train rides, alpine hiking, skiing and breathtaking lake views.

EXPLORE SWITZERLAND

Places You Must Visit

Discover the most beautiful corners of Switzerland.

Zermatt

Explore a beautiful mountain village with breathtaking views of the iconic Matterhorn.

Interlaken

Enjoy stunning lakes, mountain adventures and unforgettable outdoor experiences.

Lauterbrunnen

Discover a magical valley surrounded by towering cliffs and spectacular waterfalls.

Lucerne

Wander through charming streets, historic bridges and beautiful lakeside scenery.

Grindelwald

Experience breathtaking alpine scenery, scenic cable cars and exciting mountain activities.

Lake Geneva

Relax beside crystal-clear waters and explore picturesque towns along the lakeshore.

TRAVEL SMART

Tips for Your Swiss Adventure

A little planning goes a long way.

Getting Around

Use Switzerland's efficient trains, buses and cable cars to explore the mountains and cities.

Accommodation

Choose from cosy mountain chalets, scenic hotels and budget-friendly stays.

Budget Planning

Plan ahead for transport, meals and attractions, as Switzerland can be an expensive destination.

What to Pack

Carry comfortable walking shoes, warm layers and weather-appropriate clothing.

CHOOSE YOUR SEASON

Best Time to Visit Switzerland

Every season offers a different way to experience the Swiss Alps.

Spring

March – May

Watch the landscapes come alive with blooming flowers, green valleys and beautiful waterfalls.

Summer

June – August

Enjoy alpine hikes, scenic train rides, clear lakes and pleasant weather.

Autumn

September – November

Experience colourful forests, peaceful mountain towns and fewer tourist crowds.

Winter

December – February

Discover snowy landscapes, cosy mountain villages, skiing and festive winter markets.
