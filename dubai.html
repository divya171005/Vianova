ABOUT DUBAI

Where Modern Luxury Meets Arabian Heritage

Located in the United Arab Emirates, Dubai is a city known for its remarkable architecture, luxurious experiences, golden deserts and rich cultural traditions. From world-famous landmarks to traditional souks, Dubai offers an exciting blend of modernity and heritage.

PLAN YOUR JOURNEY

Dubai at a Glance

A few things to know before you explore.

Best Time to Visit

November – March for pleasant weather and outdoor activities.

Currency

United Arab Emirates Dirham (AED).

Language

Arabic is the official language. English is widely spoken.

Famous Experiences

Desert safaris, skyscrapers, luxury shopping and waterfront attractions.

TRAVEL SMART

Tips for Your Dubai Adventure

A little planning goes a long way.

Getting Around

Use the Dubai Metro, public buses, taxis and ride-hailing services to explore the city.

Accommodation

Choose from luxury hotels, beachfront resorts and comfortable budget-friendly stays.

Local Etiquette

Dress respectfully in traditional and religious places and be mindful of local customs.

What to Pack

Carry lightweight clothing, sunscreen, sunglasses and comfortable walking shoes.

CHOOSE YOUR SEASON

Best Time to Visit Dubai

Every season offers a different way to experience the city.

Spring

March – May

Enjoy warm weather, beach activities and outdoor attractions, especially earlier in the season.

Summer

June – August

Experience indoor attractions, shopping destinations and refreshing hotel pools during the hot months.

Autumn

September – November

Enjoy gradually cooler temperatures and the return of outdoor activities.

Winter

December – February

Explore outdoor attractions, desert safaris and waterfront experiences in pleasant weather.
