ABOUT BALI

An Island of Culture, Nature & Tranquility

Located in Indonesia, Bali is famous for its beautiful beaches, lush green landscapes, traditional temples and rich cultural heritage. Whether you seek adventure or relaxation, Bali offers something for every traveller.

PLAN YOUR JOURNEY

Bali at a Glance

A few things to know before you explore.

Best Time to Visit

April – October for sunny weather and outdoor adventures.

Currency

Indonesian Rupiah (IDR).

Language

Indonesian and Balinese. English is widely used in tourist areas.

Famous Experiences

Beach sunsets, temple visits, rice terraces, surfing and island hopping.

EXPERIENCE BALI

Things to Do in Bali

Discover experiences that make every journey unforgettable.

Explore Tropical Beaches

Relax on beautiful sandy beaches, enjoy ocean views and watch breathtaking sunsets.

Visit Ancient Temples

Discover Bali's spiritual heritage through its beautiful temples and traditional architecture.

Explore Rice Terraces

Walk through lush green rice fields and admire Bali's stunning natural landscapes.

Discover Waterfalls

Explore hidden waterfalls surrounded by tropical forests and peaceful natural scenery.

EXPLORE BALI

Places You Must Visit

Discover the most beautiful corners of Bali.

Ubud

Discover Bali's cultural heart, surrounded by lush forests, art markets and rice terraces.

Uluwatu

Enjoy dramatic coastal cliffs, beautiful beaches and spectacular ocean sunsets.

Nusa Penida

Explore stunning cliffs, turquoise waters and some of Bali's most iconic coastal views.

Seminyak

Experience stylish beach clubs, boutique shopping and lively seaside restaurants.

Kuta

Enjoy lively beaches, surfing experiences and vibrant local markets.

Tegallalang

Admire spectacular terraced rice fields and experience Bali's beautiful countryside.

TRAVEL SMART

Tips for Your Bali Adventure

A little planning goes a long way.

Getting Around

Use taxis, ride-hailing services or private drivers to explore different parts of the island.

Accommodation

Choose from tropical villas, beachfront resorts and charming boutique hotels.

Budget Planning

Plan your spending for accommodation, transport, activities and local experiences.

What to Pack

Carry lightweight clothes, sunscreen, comfortable footwear and a light rain jacket.

CHOOSE YOUR SEASON

Best Time to Visit Bali

Every season offers a different way to experience the island.

Spring

March – May

Enjoy warm weather, greener landscapes and the transition into Bali's dry season.

Summer

June – August

Experience sunny days, beach activities and excellent conditions for exploring.

Autumn

September – November

Explore the island in pleasant weather, with fewer crowds as the wet season approaches.

Winter

December – February

Discover tropical greenery and quieter surroundings, while planning around frequent rain.
