ABOUT NEW YORK

A City of Dreams, Culture & Endless Possibilities

Located in the northeastern United States, New York City is famous for its iconic skyline, diverse neighbourhoods, cultural attractions and lively atmosphere. From peaceful walks through Central Park to the bright lights of Times Square, every corner offers something new to discover.

PLAN YOUR JOURNEY

New York at a Glance

A few things to know before you explore.

Best Time to Visit

April – June and September – November for pleasant weather and sightseeing.

Currency

United States Dollar (USD).

Language

English is the most widely spoken language. Many other languages are also spoken.

Famous Experiences

Broadway shows, skyline views, shopping, museums and iconic city landmarks.

Central Park

TRAVEL SMART

Tips for Your New York Adventure

A little planning goes a long way.

Getting Around

Use the subway, city buses and walking routes to explore New York conveniently.

Accommodation

Choose from boutique hotels, comfortable apartments and centrally located stays.

Budget Planning

Plan ahead for accommodation, transport, dining and popular attractions, as New York can be expensive.

What to Pack

Carry comfortable walking shoes and season-appropriate clothing, especially during winter.

Best Time to Visit New York

Every season offers a different way to experience the city.

Spring

March – May

Enjoy blooming parks, mild temperatures and beautiful outdoor sightseeing.

Summer

June – August

Experience outdoor festivals, rooftop views and lively city streets.

Autumn

September – November

Admire colourful autumn foliage, pleasant weather and seasonal city events.

Winter

December – February

Discover festive decorations, ice skating and the magical holiday atmosphere.
